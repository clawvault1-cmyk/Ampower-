Questions, answered

What's the difference between NH1500L and NH2000L?

Continuous power: 1500W vs 2000W at 25°C, with 3000W vs 4000W peak for 2 seconds. Size and weight are the same.

What does "-BYP" mean?

Bypass models have an AC input. When shore or grid power is connected, they switch over automatically in under 25ms and pass up to 16A to your loads.

What battery do I need?

A 12V battery system (10–16 VDC input range). The NH Series is designed to work especially well with lithium batteries. Use a properly sized DC breaker between the battery and the inverter.

Will it run my US appliances?

The NH Series outputs 230/240 VAC (50/60 Hz selectable), not 120V. Contact us before ordering and we'll confirm it fits your application.

Does it charge batteries or include solar MPPT?

No. The NH Series is an inverter only. Pair it with a separate charger or solar charge controller.

Can it connect to a monitoring system?

Yes. It has RJ45 (CAN/RS485), RJ12 (LIN), D+ and remote interfaces. Output settings can also be changed through the VS28-LS display.
