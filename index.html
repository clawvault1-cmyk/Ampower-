Why NH Series

Small Box. Serious Power.

Efficiency and standby figures shown are for the International version. North American (LV) version: max 93%, 88% at full load, standby <1W.

Morning Espresso. Afternoon Zoom Call. Zero Hookups.

1500–2000W of clean pure sine wave power, with 3000–4000W of surge for appliances that kick hard at startup. Up to 93% efficient, so more of your battery goes to the things you actually use.

6.6 Pounds. Fits Where Others Won't.

At 3.0kg and 382 × 236 × 85 mm, the NH slides under a bench, behind a seat or into a cabinet. Less weight means more payload for gear. A smaller footprint means a cleaner build and a quicker install.

Plug Into Shore Power. Forget the Switch.

BYP models detect campground or generator power and hand your loads straight to it, automatically. Unplug and you're back on battery. The 120V BYP models pass through up to 20A.

Leave It On. Your Battery Won't Notice.

ECO mode keeps the inverter asleep until something asks for power, and standby draws under 1W on the 120V models. Come back after a week parked and your battery is still there for you.

Questions, answered

Does it work with US 120V appliances?

Yes. The North American (LV) models output 100/120VAC pure sine wave at 50/60Hz. Non-bypass models have a GFCI outlet.

What's the difference between standard and -BYP models?

BYP models have an AC input. When shore power or a generator is connected, your loads run directly from it (up to 20A on the North American version) and the inverter switches over automatically.

What battery do I need?

A 12V battery system. The NH Series is designed for mobile off-grid use and pairs especially well with lithium batteries. Install a properly sized DC breaker between the battery and inverter.

1500W or 2000W?

Add up the wattage of everything you'll run at once. The 1500W model handles 3000W peaks and the 2000W handles 4000W peaks (2 seconds) for motor start-ups like coffee makers, microwaves or power tools. Not sure? Send us your list and we'll size it for you.

Does it charge my battery or include solar?

No. The NH Series is an inverter only. Pair it with a separate charger or solar charge controller.

Can I monitor it from my phone?

Yes, using the LIBER43 screen or app. It also has RJ45 (CAN/RS485) and RJ12 (LIN) communication ports.
